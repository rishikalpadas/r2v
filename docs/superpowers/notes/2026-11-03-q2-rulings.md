# Q2 rulings: merge near-identical colour regions and anti-aliasing slivers (2026-11-03)

Branch `feat/quality-merge-near-colours` (from `feat/quality-defect-report` 54608c2). Code:
`engine/stages/segment_regions.py`, called from `segment()` on the quantized (noisy) path only.
Each ruling has its cost if wrong. Measurements: `eval.run_defects --enhance on` on the 21 logos
(`eval/reports/defects_q2/`, compared with `defects_baseline/`) and on `eval/dataset_synthetic`
(compared with a fresh 54608c2 run, `defects_synth_base54608/`). Report output is not committed.

## Design

Three passes on the region map, after the existing component cleanup:

1. **Near-identical merge.** Adjacent regions merge, least distinct pair (dE2000 of current
   colours) first, when (a) every pair of their members' original colours is within
   `MERGE_DELTA_E` 9 (complete linkage, no chaining along a gradient), (b) at least
   `MERGE_MIN_QUIET_SHARE` 0.5 of their shared boundary is quiet (source gradient, Lab per
   original px after a 1 original px Gaussian, below `QUIET_GRADIENT` 1.0), and (c) merging moves
   the larger region's mean *smoothed source* colour by at most `MERGE_MAX_SHIFT` 1.6 dE2000. The
   merged colour is the area-weighted Lab mean; unmerged regions keep their colour exactly.
2. **Anti-aliasing slivers.** A region whose largest inscribed distance is at most
   `SLIVER_MAX_HALF_WIDTH_PX` 1.5 original px and whose colour lies between its two
   longest-border neighbours A, B (dE(A,S) + dE(S,B) <= `SLIVER_BETWEEN_TOLERANCE` 1.5 x dE(A,B))
   is split between A and B, each pixel by its source colour; pieces cut off from their region
   flip to the other side, and a sliver that cannot be split cleanly is kept (regions stay
   4-connected).
3. **Specks.** Regions under `speck_area_px` 12 original px^2 merge into their most similar
   neighbour unless every neighbour is at least `SPECK_KEEP_DELTA_E` 15 away.

`SegmentParams` gains `region_merge_delta_e` (9.0, 0 disables the merge) and `speck_area_px`
(12.0, 0 disables), following the existing `merge_delta_e` / `min_region_px` pattern; the other
thresholds are module constants with their measurements.

## Rulings

- Ruling: quantized path only; exact-colour input (<= max_colors colours) is untouched — its
  specks and thin pieces are real source detail and the exact-path digests lock it — cost if wrong:
  none found; clean synthetic logos are byte-identical (nodes and metrics unchanged).
- Ruling: boundary contrast as a *quiet share* (fraction of boundary edges below 1.0 Lab/px), not
  the mean gradient — the mean is dominated by short stretches near outlines; measured quiet share:
  banding/noise splits 0.5-1.0, designed edges and outline-hugging pieces 0-0.3; a designed step of
  dE2000 4 reads 1.6 per px, dE2000 2 reads 0.8-1.0 — cost if wrong: a designed pale-on-pale edge
  under ~dE2000 2.5 merges (below what reads as two colours).
- Ruling: colour-shift gate on the smoothed source mean, `MERGE_MAX_SHIFT` 1.6 — a palette-based
  gate blocked equal-area halves of one fill, no gate at all flattened designed gradients (e (13)
  banner, e (10)): segment-level dE +0.06-0.08 there. 1.0 left the soft-shaded letter fills of
  e (5), e (9), e (17) split; 1.6 merges them for dE <= +0.02 per image (full pipeline) and raises
  SSIM / edge F1 — cost if wrong: genuinely shaded fills lose up to ~1.6 dE of shading.
- Ruling: `MERGE_DELTA_E` 9 — split shades of one fill measured dE2000 5.3-8.5 (the palette merge
  already joins < 5); complete linkage over original colours stops chains — cost if wrong: two
  designed colours 5-9 apart with a quiet shared boundary merge.
- Ruling: `SLIVER_BETWEEN_TOLERANCE` 1.5, not 1.2 — JPEG/SR ringing leaves edge ribbons slightly
  darker than the darker side (1.3-1.45); 1.2 kept them as ragged fragments (+75 specks, +54 wobble
  summed); real dark rims and outlines read >= 2.0 (a black outline between pink and white ~4) —
  cost if wrong: a thin stroke only a little darker than its darker neighbour is absorbed.
- Ruling: the e (11) flower rims are kept — 1 original px rims darker than both sides (ratio
  2.0-2.25), a designed offset-shadow; they hold ~90% of e (11)'s wobble (about half of all logo
  wobble). Fixing them belongs to thin-band handling (centreline + width) — cost if wrong: e (11)
  wobble stays high until then.
- Ruling: `SLIVER_MAX_HALF_WIDTH_PX` stays 1.5 (as briefed) — 2.0 gained only ~1 point on the
  four-detector sum and cost e (12) dE +0.013 — cost if wrong: 3-4 px AA ramps after SR stay.
- Ruling: sub-pixel thin tips made of blend pixels go to the nearer side by colour — on
  `aa_shapes(3)` x4 the orange tail tip is ~2 px shorter (edge F1 0.937 -> 0.916, nodes 513 -> 78);
  digest re-recorded in `tests/unit/test_crossing_guard.py` with that reason — cost if wrong:
  hairline tips under half coverage vanish.
- Ruling: speck keep threshold 15 dE2000 — i-dots and highlights contrast far more; low-contrast
  JPEG specks are < 10 — cost if wrong: a deliberate low-contrast dot under 12 px^2 is lost.
- Ruling: crossings detector tests in processing px (`CurveGeom.points_px`), as the engine's guard
  does — see "Crossings" — cost if wrong: none for the engine; detector only.

## Crossings in e (3), e (6), e (10)

- e (6) and e (10) (scale 3): false positives of the detector. A vertex of one fitted curve lies
  exactly on another curve's segment (a touch, both neighbours on one side). The guard tests in
  processing px and sees a touch; the detector divided coordinates by 3, the rounding made the
  touch two "proper" crossings. Fixed in `eval/defects.py` (test in processing px); both read 0.
- e (3) (scale 2): two real crossings between arcs that share both endpoints, overlapping by less
  than the guard's sampling error (`SAMPLE_ERROR_PX` 0.25 processing px = 0.125 original px). The
  detector samples at 0.05 original px and sees them. Not fixed: changing the guard's sampling
  changes engine output and run time; the overlap is invisible. With Q2, e (3) reads 0 anyway
  (its segmentation changed), so this remains a latent case.
